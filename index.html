How it works

  1. Click the PayPal button and approve the $29.00 Sandbox order as a Sandbox buyer.
  2. The PayPal JS SDK creates the order and captures it in the browser (Orders flow).
  3. On capture, the AI brief generator composes your onboarding brief and shows your order ID.

AI tools used: an in-browser brief-generation module (deterministic template composition driven by order data) stands in for Life Manager's production LLM manager in this hackathon prototype — disclosed honestly; the payment rail (PayPal Sandbox) is fully live.
